# Anirevia

A fast, clean anime streaming and offline viewing app for Android.

**[Download the latest Android APK](https://github.com/PatzMain/Anirevia-Mobile/releases/latest)** · **[Patch notes](CHANGELOG.md)**

## Highlights

- **Simple Navigation**: Jump straight between **Home**, **My Lists**, **Browse**, **Library**, and **Settings**.
- **Smart Discovery & Search**: Browse by genre, tag (`Historical`, `Isekai`), year, format, or status—only watchable titles are shown, with an optional 18+ toggle in Settings.
- **SUB & DUB Streaming**: Automatically picks the best server with captions in the background, and lets you switch between Japanese (SUB), English (DUB), and streaming servers right inside the video player.
- **Next Episode Countdowns**: See live release countdowns and local air times on airing series pages.
- **Seasons & Sequels**: Easily jump between seasons, prequels, sequels, and related movies directly from the watch page.
- **Upgraded Video Player**:
  - Seamless **Next Episode** playback without leaving fullscreen.
  - Double-tap to skip forward or backward (`+10s`, `+20s`, `+30s`) with haptic feedback.
  - **Swipe to Unlock** screen lock with an on-tap timeline showing intro and outro markers.
  - **Caption Style** customization with live preview for subtitle size and theme.
  - Crisp **Skip Intro** and **Skip Outro** pill buttons, plus optional swipe gestures for volume and brightness in Settings.
- **Offline Downloads & My Lists**: Save episodes to watch offline in **Library**, keep your **Watchlist** and **History** synced in **My Lists**, and install app updates right inside **Settings**.

## How to Install or Update

1. Download the APK from the **[latest release](https://github.com/PatzMain/Anirevia-Mobile/releases/latest)**:
   - **`Anirevia-Mobile-0.2.18-arm64-v8a.apk`** — Best choice for most modern Android phones.
   - **`Anirevia-Mobile-0.2.18-universal.apk`** — Works on all supported Android 7.0+ phones, tablets, and emulators.
2. Open the downloaded APK and follow Android's install prompt.

**Already have Anirevia installed?** Open **Settings → App updates** inside the app to update directly to **0.2.18**—your watchlist, watch history, settings, and downloaded episodes are all kept safe.
