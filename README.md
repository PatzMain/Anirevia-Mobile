# Anirevia

A fast, clean anime streaming and offline viewing app for Android.

**[Download the latest Android APK](https://github.com/PatzMain/Anirevia-Mobile/releases/latest)** · **[Patch notes](CHANGELOG.md)**

## Highlights

- **Fast, Clean Navigation**: Jump smoothly between **Home**, **My Lists**, **Browse**, **Library**, and **Settings** with instant cached Home loading and pull-to-refresh.
- **Smart Discovery & Search**: Browse directly into the anime catalog, with collapsible **Filters**, **Quick collections**, and **Browse by Genre**—plus an optional 18+ toggle in Settings.
- **Simple SUB & DUB Streaming**: Automatically picks the best server with captions in the background, and lets you switch between Japanese (SUB), English (DUB), and **Server 1 / 2 / 3 / 4** right inside the player.
- **Next Episode Countdowns & Seasons**: See live release countdowns on airing series and jump cleanly between seasons, prequels, sequels, and related movies.
- **Polished Video Player**:
  - Smart portrait-to-landscape auto-rotation while watching, while keeping the rest of the app cleanly in portrait.
  - Faster video startup and instant 10–30 second rewind with crisp haptic feedback.
  - Seamless **Next Episode** playback without leaving fullscreen.
  - **Swipe to Unlock** screen lock with an on-tap timeline, merged blue intro/outro highlights, and clean **Skip Intro** / **Skip Outro** pill buttons.
  - **Caption Style** customization with live preview for subtitle size and theme, plus swipe gestures for volume and brightness.
- **Fullscreen Offline Library & Easy Updates**: Watch downloaded episodes offline in fullscreen landscape from **Library**, keep **Watchlist** and **History** in **My Lists**, and update in-app from **Settings → App updates** with collapsible patch notes.

## How to Install or Update

1. Download the APK from the **[latest release](https://github.com/PatzMain/Anirevia-Mobile/releases/latest)**:
   - **`Anirevia-Mobile-0.3.0-arm64-v8a.apk`** — Best choice for most modern Android phones.
   - **`Anirevia-Mobile-0.3.0-universal.apk`** — Works on all supported Android 7.0+ phones, tablets, and emulators.
2. Open the downloaded APK and follow Android's install prompt.

**Already have Anirevia installed?** Open **Settings → App updates** inside the app to update directly to **0.3.0**—your watchlist, watch history, settings, and downloaded episodes are all kept safe.
